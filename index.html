Introduction

An overview of Kottarakkara Municipality, its regional importance and geographical setting.

Kottarakkara is an important urban centre located in Kollam district in the Indian state of Kerala. The town serves as a commercial, administrative and transport centre for its surrounding settlements.

Kottarakkara has historical and cultural significance in Kerala. The town is closely associated with Kottarakkara Thampuran, who is traditionally credited with the development of Ramanattam, an important precursor of the classical performing art Kathakali.

The municipality includes residential, commercial, institutional and agricultural landscapes. Its development reflects the interaction between physical geography, transportation networks, population distribution and changing land-use patterns.

This WebGIS platform provides a spatial perspective of Kottarakkara Municipality. It aims to support geographical understanding through interactive mapping and the visualization of spatial information.

Geographical Information

Understanding the physical and human geographical characteristics of Kottarakkara Municipality.

Location

Kottarakkara is situated in the eastern part of Kollam district, within the midland region of Kerala. It is connected to surrounding towns and rural settlements through an extensive road network.

Relief and Topography

The municipality is characterized by undulating terrain, low hills, valleys and gently sloping land. Local variations in elevation influence settlement patterns, drainage and land use.

Climate

Kottarakkara experiences a tropical monsoon climate. Rainfall is mainly received during the southwest monsoon, with additional rainfall during the northeast monsoon.

Drainage

The landscape contains local streams and drainage channels influenced by terrain and seasonal rainfall. Drainage characteristics are important for understanding surface runoff and urban flood susceptibility.

Soils and Vegetation

Lateritic soils are common across Kerala's midland region. Vegetation includes mixed homestead gardens, agricultural crops and trees associated with residential landscapes.

Land Use

Land use includes residential settlements, commercial establishments, public institutions, transport infrastructure and agricultural areas. Urban expansion contributes to changes in the existing landscape.

Transportation

Kottarakkara functions as a regional transport junction, connecting important destinations within Kollam district and neighbouring regions of Kerala.

Urban Development

The municipality has experienced changes in built-up areas, infrastructure and commercial activities. Spatial analysis helps identify patterns of urban growth and development.
